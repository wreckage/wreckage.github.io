2.0 — Who's Behind It

Meet Maria Olmos

MO

Maria has spent 14 years as a paralegal — the kind who ends up running the file room, training the new hires, and fixing the calendaring system nobody else wanted to touch. She started Olmos Legal Operations to bring that same hands-on approach to other firms: building teams, running hiring, and setting up the protocols that keep litigation support running smoothly, whether or not she's in the room.

In her day job, Maria works as a senior paralegal, where she currently manages a litigation support team of:

8 Paralegals
4 Legal Assistants
8 Associate Attorneys

Her day-to-day team — not Olmos Legal Operations staff or clients.

That hands-on experience — hiring, training, and building protocols for a team this size — is exactly what she brings to every Olmos Legal Operations engagement.
